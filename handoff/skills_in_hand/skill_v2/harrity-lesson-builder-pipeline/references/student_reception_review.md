# Student Reception Review

Assess the build from the seat of an anxious exam-focused prelicensure nursing student.

## Required checks

1. Can the student identify what to memorize?
2. Can the student identify what to recognize clinically?
3. Can the student identify the priority nursing action?
4. Are common exam traps named plainly?
5. Are answers and rationales available after questions?
6. Is the case helping comprehension, or is it distracting?
7. Does the final summary work as a study sheet?

## Score fields

- engagement_score_0_to_10
- exam_readiness_score_0_to_10
- clinical_judgment_score_0_to_10
- cognitive_load_score_0_to_10
- retention_design_score_0_to_10
- confidence_after_lecture_score_0_to_10

Any score below 7 requires a defect entry and revision recommendation.
