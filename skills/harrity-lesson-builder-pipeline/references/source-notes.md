# Source Notes and Verification

Use official or course-provided sources for clinical and exam-specific claims.

Recommended source hierarchy:

1. User-provided course text, school policy, rubric, or instructor notes.
2. Official NCSBN/NCLEX resources for CJMM and exam framing.
3. Current clinical guidelines or institutional policies for practice details.
4. Peer-reviewed or textbook sources for physiology when needed.

Do not invent current drug, screening, emergency, or policy details. Verify them when the output could affect safety, grading, or exam alignment.

The CJMM labels used by this skill are: recognize cues, analyze cues, prioritize hypotheses, generate solutions, take action, and evaluate outcomes.
