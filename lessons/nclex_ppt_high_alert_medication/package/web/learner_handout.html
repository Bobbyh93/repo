Warm-up: put the three moves in order

  • Before administration
  • During administration
  • After administration
Try it: Order the three.

Before administration

  • Verify patient identity, medication, indication, dose, route, time, allergies, relevant laboratory data, interactions
  • and required independent checks

During administration

  • Use the correct equipment and rate, minimize interruptions
  • and do not bypass smart-device or barcode safeguards without resolving the cause

After administration

  • Monitor the expected effect and predictable harms, document the response
  • and act immediately on evidence of an adverse event
