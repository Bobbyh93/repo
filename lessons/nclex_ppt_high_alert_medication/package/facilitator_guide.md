### S12 · Mini case
*mini_case · apply · source-aligned · 165 s*  
**Objective:** Respond to a cue using the lesson's frame.  
**CJM:** prioritize hypotheses, take action  
**Sources:** SRC02  

**Script:**

A bedside moment, and this time you write rather than choose. The situation: Barcode scanning produces a mismatch alert. Write two sentences before the debrief: one naming what you assess, one naming what you do about it. Keep them in that order, because the order is the judgement. The keyed reasoning is: A mismatch alert is a safety signal and should not be bypassed for convenience.

**Activity:** Two sentences: what you assess, then what you do.

**Answer key:**
- Stop and resolve the patient, medication, and order mismatch.
- A mismatch alert is a safety signal and should not be bypassed for convenience.

## Assessment items

### Q01 · mcq · before administration · recognize cues
**Stem:** The prescribed dose is inconsistent with the available concentration and usual range. What should the nurse do?  
- A. Hold administration and clarify the order before preparing the dose.
- B. Delay action until the end of the shift.
- C. Document the finding without reassessment or follow-up.
- D. Delegate the clinical judgment decision to unlicensed assistive personnel.

**Answer:** A  
**Rationale:** A dose discrepancy must be resolved before medication reaches the patient.  
*remediation → S06 · source-aligned · sources SRC02*

### Q02 · mcq · before administration · analyze cues

- A. Delay action until the end of the shift.
- B. Obtain or verify the required result before administering the medication.
- C. Document the finding without reassessment or follow-up.
- D. Delegate the clinical judgment decision to unlicensed assistive personnel.
