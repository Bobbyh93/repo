Recognize

recognize · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Recognize findings associated with a possible transfusion reaction.

  • Treat new fever, chills, dyspnea, chest or back discomfort, hypotension, urticaria
  • or other acute change during transfusion as a possible reaction until evaluated

Recognize Treat new fever, chills, dyspnea, chest or back discomfort, hypotension, urticaria, or other acute change during transfusion as a possible reaction until evaluated.

Stop and assess

stop and assess · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Sequence immediate nursing actions.

  • Stop the transfusion immediately
  • maintain venous access using new tubing and compatible solution according to policy
  • assess the patient, and obtain vital signs
