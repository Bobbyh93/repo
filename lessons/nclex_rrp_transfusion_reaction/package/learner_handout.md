**Clinical question:** Recognize new symptoms during transfusion, stop the blood product, maintain access with appropriate compatible fluid and tubing per policy, assess, and notify.

### Recognize
- Treat new fever, chills, dyspnea, chest or back discomfort, hypotension, urticaria
- or other acute change during transfusion as a possible reaction until evaluated

### Stop and assess
- Stop the transfusion immediately
- maintain venous access using new tubing and compatible solution according to policy
- assess the patient, and obtain vital signs

### Notify and investigate
- Notify the provider and transfusion service, follow emergency orders, and send the product
- tubing, or specimens as required by organizational policy
