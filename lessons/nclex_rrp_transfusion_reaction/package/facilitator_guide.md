### S12 · Mini case
*mini_case · apply · source-aligned · 165 s*  
**Objective:** Respond to a cue using the lesson's frame.  
**CJM:** prioritize hypotheses, take action  
**Sources:** SRC02  

**Script:**

A bedside moment, and this time you write rather than choose. The situation: The patient's blood pressure is falling during the reaction. Write two sentences before the debrief: one naming what you assess, one naming what you do about it. Keep them in that order, because the order is the judgement. **Answer key:**

## Assessment items

### Q01 · mcq · recognize · recognize cues
**Stem:** The patient develops chills and dyspnea shortly after a transfusion begins. What should the nurse do?  
- A. Stop the transfusion immediately and assess the patient.
- B. Delay action until the end of the shift.
- C. Document the finding without reassessment or follow-up.
- D. Delegate the clinical judgment decision to unlicensed assistive personnel.

**Answer:** A  
**Rationale:** Stopping exposure and assessing stability are the immediate priorities for a suspected reaction.
