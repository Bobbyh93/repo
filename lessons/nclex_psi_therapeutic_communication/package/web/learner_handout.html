Clinical question: Use presence, observation, open invitations, reflection, and direct safety assessment without false reassurance or judgment.

Lesson map

Warm-up: put the three moves in order

  • Create space
  • Clarify
  • Assess safety
Try it: Order the three.

Create space

  • Use calm presence, observations
  • and open invitations that allow the patient to set the pace
  • and describe their experience

Clarify

  • Reflect or restate the patient's meaning
  • and ask focused questions when safety or symptoms require clarification

Assess safety

  • Statements suggesting hopelessness or self-harm require direct
  • nonjudgmental assessment and immediate protection according to risk
