*checkpoint_mcq · clarify · source-aligned · 120 s*

**Script:**

One question, and it targets the middle of the lesson. The cue is: A grieving patient becomes silent and tearful. Read all four options and commit to one before the reveal, because the value of a checkpoint is in being wrong out loud rather than quietly. ### S12 · Mini case
*mini_case · apply · source-aligned · 165 s*  
**Objective:** Respond to a cue using the lesson's frame.  
**CJM:** prioritize hypotheses, take action  
**Sources:** SRC02  

**Script:**

A bedside moment, and this time you write rather than choose. The situation: A patient says, "Everyone is judging me." Write two sentences before the debrief: one naming what you assess, one naming what you do about it. Keep them in that order, because the order is the judgement. The keyed reasoning is: Reflection validates the expressed feeling and invites clarification.

**Activity:** Two sentences: what you assess, then what you do.

**Answer key:**
- Respond, "It sounds as though you feel judged; tell me more about that."
- Reflection validates the expressed feeling and invites clarification.

### S13 · Sort by urgency
*urgency_sort · apply · source-aligned · 165 s*  
**Objective:** Sort cues by whether they require immediate action.  
**CJM:** prioritize hypotheses  
**Sources:** SRC02  

**Script:**

Two columns, and the sorting rule is the point of the slide. Put a cue on the left when delay changes the outcome for the patient, and on the right when the correct action is verification, planning or teaching that a short delay does not worsen. Justify one of your placements aloud. Learners who can sort these reliably are the ones who prioritise well under load.

**Activity:** Place each cue and justify one.

**Answer key:**
- Act now: the cues whose keyed action is immediate
- Act in sequence: the cues whose keyed action is verification or planning

**Stem:** A patient says, "Nothing will ever get better." What should the nurse do?  
- A. Acknowledge the distress and ask directly about thoughts of self-harm.
- B. Delay action until the end of the shift.
- C. Document the finding without reassessment or follow-up.
- D. Delegate the clinical judgment decision to unlicensed assistive personnel.
