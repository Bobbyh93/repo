Decision frame

decision frame · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Differentiate tasks that require nursing judgment from tasks that may be delegated.

  • The nurse assesses the patient and task before delegating
  • Assessment, teaching, evaluation, and unstable-patient decisions remain with the licensed nurse

Decision frame The nurse assesses the patient and task before delegating. Assessment, teaching, evaluation, and unstable-patient decisions remain with the licensed nurse.

Communication

communication · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Select an appropriate delegatee using patient stability, task predictability, and local scope.

  • State the task, expected result, reporting parameters, and time frame
  • Confirm understanding and availability for questions

Communication State the task, expected result, reporting parameters, and time frame. Confirm understanding and availability for questions.

Supervision

supervision · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Provide direction, supervision, and outcome evaluation after delegation.

  • Delegation transfers performance of a task, not accountability for the nursing decision
  • Reassess the patient and evaluate completion

Supervision Delegation transfers performance of a task, not accountability for the nursing decision. Reassess the patient and evaluate completion.

Checkpoint

communication · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Choose the correct action for a single cue.

A stable patient needs routine intake and output recorded. What should the nurse do?

  1. Delay action until the end of the shift.
  2. Document the finding without reassessment or follow-up.
  3. Delegate measurement with clear reporting parameters and review the result.
  4. Delegate the clinical judgment decision to unlicensed assistive personnel.
