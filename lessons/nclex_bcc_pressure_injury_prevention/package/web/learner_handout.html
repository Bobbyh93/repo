Warm-up: put the three moves in order

  • Risk assessment
  • Prevention bundle
  • Evaluate
Try it: Order the three.

Risk assessment

  • Assess mobility, sensation, moisture, perfusion, nutrition, friction, shear, devices, and current skin findings

Prevention bundle

  • Use individualized repositioning, pressure redistribution, heel off-loading, moisture protection, nutrition support
  • and device checks

Evaluate

  • Inspect high-risk areas and device contact points, document changes
  • and revise the prevention plan when skin tolerance worsens
