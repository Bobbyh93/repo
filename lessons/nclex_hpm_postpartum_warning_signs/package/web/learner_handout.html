Warm-up: put the three moves in order

  • Expected recovery
  • Escalation
  • Teach-back
Try it: Order the three.

Expected recovery

  • Explain the usual progression of lochia, comfort measures, hydration, rest
  • and follow-up while emphasizing that symptoms must be interpreted with the whole clinical picture

Escalation

  • Urgent evaluation is needed for heavy bleeding, breathing difficulty, chest pain, seizure
  • severe headache with concerning features, or thoughts of self-harm or harming the infant

Teach-back

  • Ask the patient to explain when and how they would obtain help
  • correct gaps and include the support person when desired
