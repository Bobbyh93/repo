Anticipate exposure

anticipate exposure · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Apply standard precautions to every patient encounter.

  • Standard precautions apply to every patient
  • Add gloves, gown, mask, and eye protection according to likely contact with blood
  • body fluids, mucous membranes, nonintact skin, or contaminated equipment

Anticipate exposure Standard precautions apply to every patient. Add gloves, gown, mask, and eye protection according to likely contact with blood, body fluids, mucous membranes, nonintact skin, or contaminated equipment.

Hand hygiene

hand hygiene · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Select PPE from the anticipated exposure.

  • Perform hand hygiene at indicated moments and after glove removal
  • Gloves do not replace hand hygiene

Hand hygiene Perform hand hygiene at indicated moments and after glove removal. Gloves do not replace hand hygiene.

Exposure response

exposure response · source-aligned · sources SRC02

Respond safely to a sharps or body-fluid exposure.

  • Immediately wash or flush the exposed area, report the event
  • and follow the organization's exposure evaluation process

Exposure response Immediately wash or flush the exposed area, report the event, and follow the organization's exposure evaluation process.

Use gloves plus facial and eye protection appropriate to t PPE selection is based on the anticipated route and extent of exposure.

Perform hand hygiene immediately after glove removal. Gloves can have defects and hands can be contaminated during removal.

Wash the area, report promptly, and begin the exposure pro Immediate cleansing and timely evaluation reduce preventable harm and enable indicated prophylaxis.

Separate clean and contaminated fields before continuing c Maintaining separation limits indirect transmission.

Gloves are removed after contact with contaminated material. What should the nurse do?

  1. Delay action until the end of the shift.
  2. Document the finding without reassessment or follow-up.
  3. Perform hand hygiene immediately after glove removal.
